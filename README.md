 Mumbai Dabbawalas: Operations Management Case Study

A real-world case study for IB Business Management (Unit 5: Operations).
This is my own analysis of a public case. I have no connection to the dabbawalas.

 Summary
About 5,000 dabbawalas deliver around 200,000 home-cooked lunches a day in Mumbai
using bicycles, trains and a colour/number coding system, with almost no technology.

 Concepts applied
Lean production · Just-in-time (JIT) · Quality control · Cell production · Logistics

 Key numbers (from my Excel calculator)
- 200,000 boxes ÷ 5,000 dabbawalas = 40 boxes per person per day
- About 250 groups of 20 people, each handling about 800 boxes a day
- About 2.4 million handoffs per day

 Evaluation
- Strengths: low cost, reliability, teamwork, low environmental impact
- Limits: depends on the rail network, hard to copy in other cities
- The famous "Six Sigma" claim is an estimate, not a verified audit. My calculator
  tests how the numbers look if the real error rate were much worse.
